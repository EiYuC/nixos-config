# References

The bar layout and built-in CPU, memory, network, audio and date modules
adapt the official Polybar 3.7.2 example:
https://github.com/polybar/polybar/blob/3.7.2/doc/config.ini

The colors are selected from Catppuccin's official Mocha palette:
https://github.com/catppuccin/polybar/blob/main/themes/mocha.ini

The design adds i3 workspace indicators, Nerd Font icons, battery charge/low
states and a session menu. Hardware names BAT0 and AC match hosts/nixos.
